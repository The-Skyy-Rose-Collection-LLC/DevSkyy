# High-End Design Standard

## Discovery and direction

Document heritage, audience, price position, categories, references, commercial
goals, assets, constraints, and unknowns. The approved creative thesis must state
the brand tension, emotional target, editorial point of view, typography,
composition, color, imagery, texture, motion, three signature moves, and explicit
exclusions. Explain why the result could belong only to this brand.

## Distinctiveness gate

Score brand specificity, editorial coherence, memorability, product prominence,
interaction purpose, content credibility, and cross-route consistency from 1-5.
No dimension may score below 4 for a release candidate. Compare against at least
three relevant references and document differentiation without imitation.

## Route contracts

For each route and viewport define first impression, narrative purpose,
commercial objective, content sequence, dominant component, decision support,
primary/secondary CTA, trust content, exits, and prohibited distractions.
Acceptance criteria identify user state, trigger, expected visible behavior,
required source, failure condition, evidence method, and owner.

## Stress cases

Verify long and translated titles, mixed image ratios, missing optional media,
sparse/dense collections, sale and unavailable states, many swatches, keyboard,
touch, reduced motion, slow loading, zoom, narrow mobile, wide desktop, and RTL.
